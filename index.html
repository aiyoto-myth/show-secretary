Show Secretary

Entries, exhibit cards, prize money and cups for your show

This is an example show. Click around, print a card. Nothing here is real.

Your show's paperwork, done the evening you get the entries.

  1. Paste the class list from your schedule.
  2. Type each exhibitor's classes: 1, 4, 12-14. They get a number.
  3. Print every exhibit card, the receipts and the judges' sheets.
  4. On the day, type the placings. Prize money for each exhibitor, the cup winners, the coins you need and the newsletter results are done.

Free for shows up to 60 entries. Your show stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

Exhibit cards

One card per entry, eight to a page. Fold along the dashed line so the name faces down and the judges see only the numbers.

Receipts and judges' sheets

Receipts: each exhibitor's number, classes and fee. Judges' sheets: each class with the exhibitor numbers on the bench and boxes for the placings.

Placings

Type the exhibitor number from the judges' slip. Enter moves to the next box. Equal places: 12=15 (their prize money and points are shared).
